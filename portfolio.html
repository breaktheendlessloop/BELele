Pituitary & skull base · endoscopic endonasal surgery

Behind the nose, at the base of the skull

Pituitary tumours, craniopharyngioma, skull base meningioma and chordoma. He operates through the nose with an endoscope, without an external cut. He also organises the NUH Advanced Skull Base Masterclass.

The same surgery through the nose is used for skull base tumours in children, who are seen at the Paediatric Brain & Spine Clinic he co-founded.

Cerebral hemispheres · neuro-oncology

The brain

Brain tumours in adults. He aims to remove as much tumour as is safely possible while protecting function. Surgery is planned with cancer specialists at the National University Cancer Institute, Singapore, through the NUH Brain Tumour Multidisciplinary Clinic he leads.

Brain tumours and epilepsy in children. At the Paediatric Brain & Spine Clinic, children see the brain surgeon, the cancer doctor and the radiation doctor together.

Blood vessels of the brain

Moyamoya disease, treated with surgery that restores blood flow to the brain (revascularisation). He also treats arteriovenous malformations (AVMs), which are tangles of abnormal blood vessels.

In the news

From the operating theatre

The Straits Times · June 2024

The “miracle baby” with a brainstem tumour

Baby E was born in 2022 with a high-grade glioma in her brainstem, and her parents were told nothing could be done. At NUH, with Dr Nga as the neurosurgeon in charge, the team biopsied the tumour when she was about a week old.

The tumour then stopped growing, and within six months it had shrunk by half. At two years old she was thriving, possibly the first documented child to survive such a tumour.

“We believe that the tumour regression, in this particular case, is related to the biopsy that was performed.”

Published as “Rare regression of congenital brainstem high-grade glioma”, Child’s Nervous System.

The Straits Times · January 2026

Saving face: 20 years of craniofacial care

A young man with Crouzon syndrome, a rare condition that fuses the skull’s growth joints too early, has had four major operations since he was 18 months old. Dr Nga was the neurosurgeon on the NUH team that reshaped his skull as an adult, and he monitored his brain development over the years.

“Any injury to the dura or the blood channels within it could be catastrophic.”

The patient, now 23, is training for his first half-marathon.

Every patient is different. Your NUH team will explain your own options.

“To see them pull through their darkest moment and recover from their cancer, stroke or head injury, it is immensely rewarding.”
Dr Vincent Nga

    Innovation · design patent

    A burr hole cover, from idea to international market

    He designed a burr hole cover that lets surgeons place a drain easily after draining a chronic subdural haematoma. Data show it improves how the scar looks and how patients feel and function. It is design-patented, commercialised and available internationally.
