About

From Sitiawan to the operating theatre

Dr Vincent Nga grew up in Sitiawan, Perak, Malaysia. Today he heads neurosurgery in one of Singapore's leading academic health systems.

He earned his medical degree from the National University of Singapore in 2007. An Academic Medicine Development Award in 2014 took him to Addenbrooke's Hospital, Cambridge, for two years of training. There he worked across paediatric neurosurgery, neuro-oncology, neurovascular, neurotrauma and complex spine surgery. He then completed a clinical fellowship in paediatric neurosurgery at The Hospital for Sick Children (SickKids) in Toronto.

Besides operating, he trains the next generation of neurosurgeons as a residency programme director. He also completed an Executive MBA at NUS, where Poets&Quants named him one of the Best & Brightest of 2023.
