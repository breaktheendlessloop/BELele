Why It Works — Behavioural Science

Five nudges. All proven.

⚙️

Default Effect

Thaler & Sunstein, 2008 · Nobel Prize Economics 2017

Sustainable option appears first and largest. Must actively reject it to choose the worse option.

📉

Loss Aversion

Kahneman & Tversky, 1979 · Nobel Prize Economics 2002

"Save $0.30 + avoid 0.06kg CO₂" — framed as loss avoided. We feel losses 2× more intensely than gains.

👥

Social Norms

Cialdini, 1984 · Influence: The Psychology of Persuasion

ACS school leaderboard — comparing with classmates you know drives the strongest behaviour change.

🌳

CO₂ Equivalents

Abstract-to-Concrete Translation · Cognitive Accessibility Research

0.06kg CO₂ means nothing. "Not driving 300 metres" means something. We always translate numbers to reality.

🔥

Streak Logic

Habit Loop Theory · Duhigg, The Power of Habit, 2012

Breaking a 14-day streak hurts more than maintaining it helps. Turns conscious choices into automatic habits.
